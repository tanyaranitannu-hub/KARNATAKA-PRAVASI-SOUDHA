About Karnataka Pravasi Soudha

Welcome to Karnataka Pravasi Soudha, Tirumala, your serene retreat in the divine hills of Tirumala. We are dedicated to providing a peaceful and comfortable stay for pilgrims visiting the sacred shrine of Lord Venkateswara.

Nestled amidst the tranquil surroundings of Tirumala, Karnataka Pravasi Soudha offers a perfect blend of spirituality, comfort, and convenience. Our well-maintained accommodations cater to the needs of devotees, ensuring a hassle-free and restful experience during their pilgrimage.
